# fitmyface-media

**Want to try a look on your own face?** [Open FitMyFace — free hairstyle and glasses try-on](https://fitmyface.pages.dev/?src=medrepo). Choose haircuts or eyewear, then use your own photo if you wish. No account required. AI previews are illustrative, and the service is for adults outside Illinois.

This is the public media repository for the [FitMyFace browser tool](https://fitmyface.pages.dev/?src=medrepo), not an account sign-up or a retailer. The `medrepo` parameter is an existing anonymous link-source label to help measure whether this page actually brings visitors; it does not include photo or personal data.


## Find the right look or solve a real buying decision

These are existing FitMyFace pages, not a requirement to sign up. Use the free adult browser tool to explore before making a haircut or eyewear purchase; AI previews are illustrative and cannot verify in-person fit or guarantee a haircut.

- **[Try hairstyles on your own photo](https://fitmyface.pages.dev/guides/try-hairstyles-on-your-photo)** — what a photo try-on can show before a haircut and where its limits are.
- **[Compare glasses across stores](https://fitmyface.pages.dev/guides/compare-glasses-across-stores)** — investigate frame styles and browse public retailer catalogs; check availability with the merchant and physical fit separately.
- **[Should I get bangs?](https://fitmyface.pages.dev/guides/should-i-get-bangs)** — compare fringe options before a commitment.
- **[What to tell your barber](https://fitmyface.pages.dev/guides/what-to-tell-your-barber)** — turn a visual idea into a more specific conversation about the cut.
- **[How glasses frame numbers work](https://fitmyface.pages.dev/guides/glasses-frame-size)** — explain lens, bridge and temple measurements before you order.

For the actual try-on, [open FitMyFace](https://fitmyface.pages.dev/?src=medrepo). Free use has daily AI-capacity limits. Ages 18+; unavailable in Illinois. This page is maintained for useful discovery, not to imply an independent endorsement or customer result.

Public download links for **finished, approved** FitMyFace social videos, so the Buffer scheduler can fetch them
(Buffer needs a public file URL; the main repo is private). Every file here is already meant to be public on
TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. Faces are fictional and AI-made; looks are AI previews.

Rules: only videos that passed independent review and are not held (see `docs/MARKETING_STATUS.md` in the main repo).
No raw photos, no user photos, no credentials, nothing else.

`legal/` holds the public privacy policy and data-deletion instructions for the FitMyFace Studio publishing tool (required by the YouTube, Meta and TikTok developer programs). No personal data.
