# FitMyFace Studio: privacy policy

*Last updated: 2026-11-04*

FitMyFace Studio is the internal tool the FitMyFace team uses to publish **our own** short videos to **our own** FitMyFace accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, and to read those accounts' post performance. It is not offered to the public and does not sign in other people's accounts.

## What we access
- Identifiers of our own FitMyFace social accounts and of the videos we posted.
- Aggregate performance numbers for those videos: views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves, watch time and retention.
- Nothing about individual viewers. We do not collect, receive or store personal data about people who watch, like or comment.

## What we do not do
- We do not access any account other than FitMyFace's own.
- We do not sell, rent or share this data. It is used only to decide which of our own video formats work better.
- We do not use the data for advertising or profiling of individuals.

## Storage and security
- Access tokens are kept as encrypted secrets in our cloud environment, never in source code or public files.
- Performance numbers are kept for up to 24 months, then deleted.

## Revoking access and deletion
- YouTube: Google's Privacy Policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy. By using YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
- Instagram and TikTok: access can be removed in each app's settings (Apps and websites / Manage app permissions).
- How to ask us to delete data: see [DATA_DELETION.md](DATA_DELETION.md).

## Website
This policy covers only the publishing tool. The FitMyFace website's own privacy notice is at https://fitmyface.pages.dev/privacy.html.
