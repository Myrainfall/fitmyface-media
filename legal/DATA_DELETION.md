# FitMyFace Studio: data deletion instructions

*Last updated: 2026-11-04*

FitMyFace Studio only reads aggregate numbers about FitMyFace's **own** posts. It stores no personal data about viewers or other users, so in most cases there is nothing about you to delete.

If you believe we hold data connected to you, or you want our app's access to an account removed:

1. **Remove the app's access yourself (immediate):**
   - Instagram / Facebook: Settings → Apps and websites → remove "FitMyFace Studio".
   - TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services → remove "FitMyFace Studio".
   - Google / YouTube: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove access.
2. **Ask us to delete stored data:** open an issue at https://github.com/Myrainfall/fitmyface-media/issues with the title "Data deletion". Do not post personal details publicly; just say which platform. We delete any matching data within 30 days and confirm in the issue.

When access is removed, we delete the related access token and stop collecting within 24 hours.
